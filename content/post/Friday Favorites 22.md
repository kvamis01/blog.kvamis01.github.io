Happy Friday,

[Cue the music](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3L4YrGaR8E4), brew that pot. One of my favorite camping spots in northern Minnesota had an AQI above 1,600 this week, and my mouth tastes smoky while I’m typing this many hours south of there.

Here are 10 interesting things worth sharing this week:

1. [PurpleAir](https://www2.purpleair.com/) is my favorite way to track air quality and monitor the summer smoke situation, recommended by someone who’s dealt with more wildfires in Colorado.
2. “There is such a thing as righteous anger, because that is not about you and your personal ego; it really is the anger you’re feeling on the behalf of the vulnerable.” Anger can be a powerful, empowering, and positive force when facing [systemic injustices like climate change](https://app.notion.com/p/Why-I-m-Staying-Angry-About-Climate-Change-The-Atlantic-14058d87ea8b486aa5474635c0937af8?pvs=21). (It’s been my main climate emotion for 7 years and I recommend it.)
3. I’m also a fan of having a [creative nemesis](https://austinkleon.com/tag/nemesis/). (My former nemesis wrote climate disinformation in Minnesota.)
4. “’It’s much worse than we think, and sooner;’ [the child in my dream said] to whom I said ‘no duh child in my dreams, what do you think this singing and shuddering is, what this creaming and reaching and dancing and crying is, other than loving what every second goes away?” Ross Gay’s “[Catalog of Unabashed Gratitude](https://wordsfortheyear.com/2018/02/25/catalog-of-unabashed-gratitude-by-ross-gay/)” is one of my favorite poems, and it’s great set to [Bon Iver](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EBWcnGjfadY).
5. Adults are [seven times likelier](https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2023/aug/21/anger-is-most-powerful-emotion-by-far-for-spurring-climate-action-study-finds) to be advocating/taking action against climate change if their primary emotion is anger rather than hope. For every two clicks a person moves on the anger scale, they move one click on the activism scale.
6. I’ve apparently replaced “Big Book Summer” with “Stephen King Summer” after finishing “[The Body](https://bookshop.org/p/books/different-seasons-four-novellas-stephen-king/7b1ff60130e8ad4c?ean=9781501143489&bkshp-astro=t)” now listening to “[Duma Key](https://bookshop.org/p/books/duma-key-a-novel-stephen-king/09ffa20fc0e5e1e1)” and reading “[Fairy Tale](https://bookshop.org/p/books/fairy-tale-stephen-king/452797da1cfdb3be?ean=9781668002193&bkshp-astro=t).” Something about King’s characters, from 11-year-old boys to high school teenagers to adults, makes his stories interesting even when the plot lags or the endings are unfulfilling. I grew up in a small town, and there’s something genuine in his simple, rural characters.
7. “Humans are not as dysfunctional as we think, we can work together to produce something joyful.” [David Byrne](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2026/jul/09/david-byrne-reader-interview-touring-talking-heads-ramones-lou-reed) on why he replaced one drummer with six so they can move around on stage.
8. I’m fascinated by people who commit to a seemingly pointless and time-intensive project , like [Van Neistat typewriting](https://m.youtube.com/watch?v=QlRtFugjQtw&ra=m), cutting out, and arranging every word of Kurt Vonnegut’s “Slaughterhouse Five” or the [local legend](https://racketmn.com/i-rode-valley-fairs-excalibur-rollercoaster-twice-with-superfan-excalibur-michael) riding Valley Fair’s Excalibur Roller Coaster (my childhood favorite) over and over, day after day, in search of — something.
9. When I grew up, the internet was a _place_; specifically, a computer tower and monitor on a desk in a room in our house. Now it’s in my pocket (and seemingly in my hand without me noticing), so I like [this idea](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/14/opinion/culture/phone-algorithm-addiction.html) of using a scrolly phone and a scrolly chair to make it place-based again.
10. Very excited that Adventure Journal, a quarterly print mag that’s my ride or die (been subscribing since Issue 18 and want to buy the first 17) has archived their entire catalog to [subscribers](https://www.adventure-journal.com/).
