Happy Friday,

[Cue the music](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ljKvMKx1BgY), brew that pot. I’m sending this newsletter out before killing time at my local bookstore before catching a matinee of “The Odyssey” — an ideal way to start the weekend early.

Here are 10 interesting things worth sharing this week:

1. I’ve been listening to Charli xcx’s “[No One Lasts Forever](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ly3kGDyoNU8)” on repeat since Tuesday, the one with filmmaker David Cronenberg talking about death, mortality, and art. Goosebumps. So I loved [this deep dive](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_OhDEvEODsc) into the song with Charli, AG Cooke, and Finn Keane. I assumed they sampled an interview, but Charli personally recorded him! It reminded me of Steve Reich’s “[It’s Gonna Rain](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X1zuX6nRHNk)” and “[Different Trains](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ajJYZKDhHfo),” and it was neat to hear AG Cooke confirm it.
2. Seriously impressed by this person’s dedication to building a [typewriter-inspired writerdeck](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MEqnDIuDGxc), complete with 3-D printing a typewriter shell, creating an LED word counter, and a sliding screen. Puts my writerdeck (ViWoods e-reader plus a clacky keyboard) to shame!
3. I don’t take for granted how great music streaming is — you can listen to anything, whenever you want! A miracle. But there’s also too much to choose from, so I’ve been enjoying curated radio shows more: [American Routes](https://www.amroutes.org/2025-1), [David Byrne Radio](https://davidbyrne.com/radio), [Mark Hoppus’ After Hours Radio](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/after-school-radio/id1704200775), and the weekly [Bob Dylan radio hour](https://www.thenorth1033.org/show/highway-61-revisited) on 103.3.
4. I might have to find the 37-volume set of Pliny writing about the natural world in the first century A.D. to dip in and out of like [Valeria Luiselli suggests](https://archive.is/20260723140437/https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/23/books/review/valeria-luiselli-beginning-middle-end.html). Same but different: I snagged a copy of NYRB’s “[The Journal](https://bookshop.org/p/books/the-journal-of-henry-david-thoreau-1837-1861-henry-david-thoreau/10d35389f92ea32d?ean=9781590173213&bkshp-astro=t)” from Thoreau, a collection of entries about the natural world — its [secrets and experiences](https://blog.isakkvam.com/post/strenuous-or-relaxing-time-off/).
5. One of the [best openers](https://defector.com/abandoned-fish-sauce-canada-interview?giftLink=505b5928ed86e26ec35f7ee29154942c) I’ve read in a while: “Take capelin, a small silvery fish in the smelt family. Now add salt. Then throw those two ingredients in 110 vats, each of them about 10 feet tall, and let them sit in a defunct factory for over two decades. The resulting stench might haunt an entire community, as it has the people of St. Mary's, a coastal town of about 300 people in the Canadian province of Newfoundland and Labrador.”
6. I’ve been enjoying ending my day by reading a snippet of this [New Yorker essay](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2026/07/06/did-a-climber-leave-his-girlfriend-to-die-at-the-top-of-a-mountain) about an international scandal and “Alpine Divorce” — a bonkers story for outdoorsy folks.
7. “But there is a value, too, in language that dissolves like an afternoon in July, the last rays guttering in the branches as you close your book and go inside.” Great writing about the “[Beach Reads](https://www.newyorker.com/books/page-turner/the-invention-of-the-beach-read)” here — but I’d be lying if I didn’t say summer and winter are for big books! Something about the long seasons around the solstice make me want a long story, not an episodic one.
8. In a huge loss for Minnesotans and people with good taste, Pearson’s — of Salted Nut Roll fame — [is closing](https://www.startribune.com/pearsons-candy-factory-closing-salted-nut-bar-nut-goodie-bar/601873708) in Minnesota. I await an in-depth, 3,000-word tribute to the snack and will report back.
9. I’m more of a “scribble it in a field notes notebook” type of guy but I very much love the appeal of creating a [digital garden](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0tY7Z53QJo8) to have some semblance of organizing what I read online.
10. And finally, [30 seconds of David Lynch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5NVMeVB5Q-Q) to end your week: “Stay alert, do your work, don’t worry about the world going by; it doesn’t mean you can sit around and not do anything, you’ve got to get your butt in gear and do it.”
