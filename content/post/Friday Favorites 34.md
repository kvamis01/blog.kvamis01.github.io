Happy Friday,

[Cue the music](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XQ-B_sVr7TA), brew that pot. I had a productive week of owling: a great horned, two barred, a northern saw-whet, and TWO long-eared owls (firsts for me) swooped over me a dozen times the day before the full moon. Unreal!

Here are 10 interesting things worth sharing this week:

1. Chat Pile is loud, sludgy band with songs about American ugliness, and they ripped on their [recent KEXP set](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tMJbTmNbeMc), mostly thanks to singer Raygun Busch’s performance. I read a spot-on Instagram comment that Raygun brings mad toddler energy (in a good way!) to his shows, complete with not wearing shoes or a shirt when onstage.
2. As an avid Field Notes daily user since 2019, I’m stoked the gang brought back a [pocket planner](https://fieldnotesbrand.com/products/daybook) just in time for the new year. (I loved their [Ignition series](https://fieldnotesbrand.com/products/ignition) from 2021.)
3. New Field Notes has me daydreaming about ordering a bulk, custom order of the iconic [Bic Clic Stic pens](https://www.bicclic.com/product-details/p18/retro-pen-premium) you used to see in restaurants and businesses when you were a kid (now Bic is calling them “retro pens?”). While you noodle on what to put on your custom order, read this [ode to the iconic pen.](https://www.theoldghosts.com/stories/the-best-pen-can-only-be-stolen/)
4. Bob Dylan’s “[Self Portrait](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qKCepuQ-W_k&list=PLKZWLu6q09LNIov8Z7Xz-zBoZpeJH6jTW)” is one of his weirder albums with very different sounds. I didn’t know Mumford’s “[The Boxer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JDZ_t733XP8)” was a cover from it! “Being a Bob fan feels like [being in on an inside joke](https://stereogum.com/2318956/agriculture-on-how-zen-buddhism-lou-reeds-transformer-bob-dylan-more-influenced-new-album-the-spiritual-sound/interviews) that you're actually not in on, but you can pretend to be in on.”
5. Anything related to wellness or productivity has gotten shoveled into “should” and “to-do” land for me. Most of it feels like I can never do enough of it or do it the right way for it to be “right.” So this article about how [optimization has ruined wellness](https://app.notion.com/p/The-Era-of-Constant-Self-Improvement-Is-Finally-Ending-Here-s-What-s-Replacing-It-f41a761882a042988014e63363f98afa?pvs=21) was thought-provoking. (I want [Jia Tolentino](https://www.newyorker.com/contributors/jia-tolentino) to write a cultural essay about this please.)
6. Love [scanning through](https://apps.npr.org/best-books/#tags=sci+fi%2C+fantasy+%26+speculative+fiction&view=covers&year=2026) annual book [roundups](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/23/books/review/notable-books.html) and waiting for people to disagree about what was and wasn’t included.
7. Louis L’Amour wrote a [great memoir](https://bookshop.org/p/books/education-of-a-wandering-man-a-memoir-louis-l-amour/4b3e472d1d31301b?ean=9780553286526) about reading, writing, and traveling — so I’ve really enjoyed his son [Beau making videos](https://www.instagram.com/p/DgIeKy3gUvf/) about him, his books, and what he’s learned about writing.
8. “[You may just need to learn how to really try hard!](https://masoncurrey.substack.com/p/how-to-try-hard-in-writing-and-art)”
9. A few times a year a big publication will write about how [typewriters are still useful](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/22/insider/typewriter-artificial-intelligence.html), and I read them every time. Pretty cool how these products are 50-100 years old, still work as they were meant, and keep getting passed from new owner to new owner.
10. Everyone’s talking about GTAVI, but I don’t have a PS5, so I’m mostly thinking about how they managed to get SO MANY musicians to collab on its [game soundtrack](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hYvK824FQK8&list=PLQZuxG28VdTo).
